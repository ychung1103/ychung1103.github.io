Goals 

             1. Understand the principles of Computer Architecture and their implementation details.

             2. Use hardware description language to design a simplified MIPS CPU.

             3. Use software to design an assembler and a simulator of MIPS CPU.

1.      Computer Organization and Design: The Hardware/Software Interface (5th Edition), 2014 (PDF)

         David Patterson and John Hennessy

2.     Introduction to Computing Systems: From Bits & Gates to C & Beyond (2nd Edition), McGraw-Hill © 2004

                    Yale N. Patt and Sanjay J. Patel
