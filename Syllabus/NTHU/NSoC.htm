Syllabus for Networked SoC Systems (CS5104)

　

Instructor : 鍾 葉 青 (Yeh-Ching Chung)

Office : CSEE635

Phone: Ext. 2971

Goals : 瞭解連網型系統晶片系統及相關之嵌入式系統軟體。並以PCM7230、PreSOC及Google Android phone為實習平台，發展Google Android phone相關應用軟體。

Textbooks :

1.  Compuers as Components - Princple of Embedded Computing System Design, Wayne Wolf, Morgan Kaufamann Publishers, 2000.

2.  Real-Time Systems and Programming Languages (Third Edition), Alan Burns and Andy Wellings, Addison Wesley Longmain , 2001.

References :

1.  Embedded System Design - A Unified Hardware/Software Introduction, Frand Vahid and Tony Givargis, John Wiley & Sons, Inc., 2002.

Lecture Contents:

-        Embedded Systems (es-1, es-2, es-3, es-4, es-5, es-6)

-        Real-Time Operating Systems (RTOS-1, RTOS-2, RTOS-3, RTOS-4)

-        Real-Time Programming (RTP-1, RTP-2, RTP-3)

-        Networks for Embedded Systems (network-1, network-2, network-3)

-        Real-Time Networking (slides)

-       Power Management and Low Power Design of for Embedded Processors (slide-1, slide-2, slide-3)
